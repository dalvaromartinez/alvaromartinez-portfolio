Brain Tumor MRI Classification

Computer vision workflow for binary MRI scan classification using augmentation and convolutional neural networks.

PythonTensorFlowKerasNumPyMatplotlib
This project helps the portfolio feel broader than tabular ML alone by showing an imaging pipeline and basic deep learning experimentation.
No dedicated visual cover was available for this case study, so the page focuses on method, structure and linked deliverables.

What this case shows

  • Train, validation and test handling to maintain cleaner experimental structure.
  • Image augmentation to increase robustness when working with a limited dataset.
  • CNN training with monitoring and practical controls such as early stopping.
  • Evaluation through standard classification metrics and review of prediction behavior.

Project positioning

It is a useful breadth project: not necessarily the strongest flagship, but important to show that the portfolio is not limited to spreadsheets and classical ML.
